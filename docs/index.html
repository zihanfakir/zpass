Engineered for Privacy & Convenience

A complete security suite that runs entirely on your device.

🔐

Zero-Knowledge Vault

Every credential, note, card, and custom field is individually encrypted locally before hitting disk. Your Master Password is never transmitted or written to unencrypted storage.

⚡

Built-In 2FA Authenticator

Full RFC 6238 compliant TOTP token generator with live animated countdown circles, 6-digit dynamic tokens, and one-tap auto-clearing clipboard copy.

🎲

CSPRNG & Diceware Generator

Generate high-entropy random passwords or memorable multi-word Diceware passphrases with custom separators and real-time crack time estimates.

👆

Biometric & PIN Quick Unlock

Hardware-backed Android Keystore enclave (TEE/StrongBox) enables fingerprint, facial recognition, and quick PIN unlock without caching the master password in RAM.

🛡️

Defense in Depth

Window FLAG_SECURE blocks screen capture and task switcher previews. Ephemeral memory is zeroized on lock, and clipboard memory auto-purges after 30 seconds.

📦

Encrypted Backups & Import

Export full vault backups encrypted with a dedicated recovery passphrase (.openvault). Full compatibility with Bitwarden JSON and standard CSV formats.

The OpenVault Privacy Manifesto

We believe that personal privacy and strong cryptography are fundamental human rights. In an era where password managers aggressively monetize credentials, OpenVault remains free from corporate venture capital, free from tracking SDKs, and free forever.
